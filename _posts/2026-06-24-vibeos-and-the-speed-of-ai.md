Recently I came across [VibeOS](https://github.com/kaansenol5/VibeOS) when I was wasting time scrolling Xiaohongshu. It is an operating system hallucinated with Claude Code. It is not like vibe-coding a frontend, a backend, or something that still follows familiar software development patterns. Here, almost everything feels like hallucination. The LLM is pretending to be an operating system and playing with users. It means every time you open the same page, you may get a different view, thanks to today's LLM's next token prediction essence. There are also some funny and slightly chaotic reports around similar vibe-coded OS projects, like the ones from [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/software/operating-systems/ai-vibe-coded-operating-system-is-so-bad-it-cant-even-run-doom-vib-os-cant-connect-to-the-internet-browser-app-is-an-image-viewer) and [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/software/operating-systems/this-vibe-coded-operating-system-looks-like-a-nightmarish-version-of-our-ai-os-future-although-some-of-the-function-keys-change-the-wallpaper-so-thats-fun/).

What surprised me was the feeling of time collapsing.

Two years ago, when I was grinding ECE391 in Champaign's brutal winter, operating systems felt almost sacred to me. You had to understand memory, interrupts, scheduling, filesystems, devices, and a hundred small details that punish you when you pretend to understand them. Looking back from today, some of that workflow feels almost primitive: reading docs, using GDB to debug, staring at weird crashes, building things slowly by hand.

Maybe the point is not whether it is a good OS right now. Maybe the point is that an OS can start to become less like a fixed machine and more like a generated world. Maybe one day people will be kept like factory-farmed chickens, each lying inside a coffin-like pod, tubes feeding the mouth and nose, sensors pressed against the forehead, while AI hallucinates a grand, colorful, dramatic life directly into the mind. Software no longer as a tool we operate, but as an environment that operates on us.
